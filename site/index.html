Pattern

Scrawl paints Warli, Gond and sketchbook art from a number. Nothing is stock and nothing is clip art: every mark is generated, so change the number and you get a hand that has never drawn before.

01 · Same subject

Same deer.
Opposite rules.

An art tradition is a set of rules, not a set of shapes. Warli and Gond draw the same deer, the same peacock, the same water carrier, and they look nothing alike, because what sits underneath is opposite. Pick a subject.

02 · The traditions

Each one is a discipline,
not a folder of clip art.

Switching tradition swaps everything at once: the grounds and pigments it actually used, how the brush behaves, how a shape is filled, and which pieces are on offer. Click a piece to redraw it. Click a swatch to change the wall.

03 · The hand

Controlled
imperfection.

The hand-drawn feeling is not a style you can copy from a PNG. Clean geometry goes in, a seeded pen corrupts it, and SVG comes out. A straight line bows a little, a circle overshoots its join, a shape is drawn twice with a small offset.

Or, for a tradition that paints with confidence, almost no wobble at all. Each tradition ships its own hand. Try them, then break them.

04 · Seeds

4,294,967,296
hands per piece.

Every piece is a generator locked to a number. Same seed, same drawing, forever. A new seed is a new hand drawing the same thing. Turn the dials too, and the drawing itself changes.

05 · Starting points

Start from a
finished wall.

Every template is laid out the way its tradition composes. Warli has no hero and no foreground, so its layouts weave across the whole surface. Gond puts one creature in the middle and lets the fill speak. Open one, and every piece in it is still live.

06 · Under the paint

Small, open,
and yours.

Vector the whole way

Every piece is SVG from the first mark. It prints, cuts on a vinyl cutter, scales to a billboard, and stays a few kilobytes. Export PNG, SVG, or a print file with bleed and crop marks.

Nothing leaves your browser

No account, no server, no upload. Open the page and draw. Your work saves itself in the browser as you go, and a project file takes it anywhere else.

Roles, not colours

Pieces draw with roles like line, accent and fill. Change the palette and every drawing on the board recolours itself, so a brand kit takes one click.

A tradition in four files

Generators, presets, templates and one row of rules. Find the tradition's one mechanism and build on it: Warli's is a table of joints, Gond's is a swept tube.
