Paint with a number

Move your cursor to paint.Tap to paint.

Scrawl is a drawing machine for folk art. Nothing in it is clip art. Every mark is generated from a rule and a number, so the same seed gives the same drawing forever, and a new one gives you a hand that has never drawn before. It all runs in your browser and comes out as clean vector.

Same deer.Opposite rules.

Warli and Gond both draw the deer, the peacock and the water carrier, and they look nothing alike, because the rules underneath are opposite. Drag the line.
