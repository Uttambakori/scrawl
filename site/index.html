Traditions

Six traditions. Each with its own rules.

A tradition in Scrawl is not a sticker pack. It is a discipline: the grounds and pigments artists actually used, how the brush moves, and how a shape gets filled. Click a drawing to redraw it.

A library that draws itself.

Every piece is a small program, not a picture. Change a dial or a seed and it draws again, in the same hand.

How it works

From a blank page to print in three steps.

  1. 01

    Pick a tradition

    Every tradition sets its own ground, pigments and brush. Switch it and the whole page follows.

  2. 02

    Make it yours

    Drop in pieces, shuffle the seed, turn the dials. The same seed always gives the same drawing.

  3. 03

    Export anywhere

    PNG, JPG and WebP on every plan. Pro adds SVG, 4× and print PDF with bleed and crop marks.
